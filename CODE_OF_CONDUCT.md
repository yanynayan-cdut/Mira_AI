# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and constructive community. Participate with consideration for differing experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.

Examples of expected behavior include clear and constructive feedback, accepting corrections gracefully, respecting boundaries, and focusing discussion on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related community spaces. The repository maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not post private information or sensitive allegations in a public issue.
